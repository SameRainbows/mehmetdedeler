I'm 14, making apps, simulations and tools. Here are the five biggest.

Projects

  1. Typd2026

    Voice dictation for Windows and Apple-silicon Macs. One small model on your computer hears you and writes the finished text. After a one-time model download it works offline, and your voice leaves the computer only if you choose to share it.

    Free, and the app is open source. On 70 real dictations of my own, scored against texts written in Typd's style, Typd got 4.2% of the spoken words wrong and Wispr Flow got 6.1%. One speaker is a small sample.

    DownloadDetails and resultsCode

  2. Kairos2026

    An Android app for reading quotes, each one credited to a source you can check.

    6,500 quotes, 1,100 checked word for word against the original books. In closed testing on Google Play; email me to join.

  3. Tally2026

    Counts your reps through your camera, in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Nine exercises, counted on your device.

    Try itCode

  4. Orbital2026

    A gravity sandbox in Python. Slingshot planets and the orbits come from gravity alone.

    Released for Windows, macOS and Linux (v1.0.2).

    DownloadCode

  5. Formicai2026

    Ant colonies whose behaviour is evolved, not programmed. Each ant runs a small neural network.

    Released for Windows, macOS and Linux (v1.2).
