Projects

  1. TypdWindows · macOS

    Voice dictation for Windows (NVIDIA GPU) and Apple-silicon Macs. A single local model hears you and writes the finished text — no second model to clean it up. After a one-time model download it works offline, and your voice leaves the computer only if you choose to share it.

    Free, and the app is open source. Built on Qwen3-ASR 1.7B fine-tuned for dictation, running in llama.cpp (~0.5 s on short dictations on a 4 GB laptop).

  2. KairosAndroid · Testing

    An Android app for reading quotes, each one credited to a source you can check.

    6,500 quotes, 1,100 checked word for word against the original books. In closed testing on Google Play; email me to join.

  3. TallyWeb

    Counts your reps through your camera, in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

    Nine exercises, tracked with an on-device pose model. If a rep doesn't count, it says why.

  4. OrbitalPython · Desktop

    A gravity sandbox built with only Python's standard library — no physics engine. Slingshot planets and the orbits come from gravity alone.

    Continuous collision detection and momentum conservation. Released for Windows, macOS and Linux (v1.0.2).
